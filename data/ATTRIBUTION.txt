Original experiment:
Brooks JP, Edwards DJ, Harwich MD Jr, et al. (2015).
The truth about metagenomics: quantifying and counteracting bias in 16S
rRNA studies. BMC Microbiology 15:66.
https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-015-0351-6

Data collation and multiplicative calibration framework:
McLaren MR, Willis AD, Callahan BJ (2019).
Consistent and correctable bias in metagenomic sequencing experiments.
eLife 8:e46923. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.46923

Analysis transformations: retain seven named taxa; exclude the Other
category and condition depth on retained reads. Do not remove reads from
nominally absent named taxa. Use the unique seven-taxon mixture on each
plate as calibration and the other 39 mixtures on that plate as targets.
Nominal actual compositions are normalized to sum to one. No raw FASTQ
files, additional personal data, or independent measurements are used.
